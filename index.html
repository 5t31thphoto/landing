// mantissec

See the signals everyone else ignores.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are all around you, all the time. These tools make that invisible traffic something you can watch, map and understand.

CsiRadar32

See who's in the room. Without a camera.

Our full spy kit. Tiny beacons fill a room with Wi-Fi, receivers read how those signals bend around moving bodies, and after a quick calibration walk you get a live top-down map of movement in the space. Nothing calls home and nothing sees through your walls. It's just Wi-Fi, read like light.

  1. Flash every board from your browser
  2. Set the beacons around the room
  3. Walk the 90-second calibration
  4. Watch the live map

// who we are

Who's behind the mantis

BasaltSoftworks is a small family team. We build firmware to solve our own problems, then share it with our friends. Now that includes you.

We make games and tools for people who would rather be out in the real world than stuck in the cloud or lost in a feed. Everything runs on hardware you can hold, and every project is built to be customized, because tinkering with your device is part of owning it.

If any of this reminds you of the best era of tech, with real gadgets that had personality and secrets worth finding, that's not an accident.

  • Learn the real thingFlashing microcontrollers, how firmware works, how networks and games actually tick. We show you how it all works without killing the magic.
  • Offline firstYour device works for you, not for a server somewhere else.
  • Everything connectsOur projects talk to each other, share progress and reward you for exploring.
  • Secrets includedPoke around. Some of the best stuff isn't in any menu.
